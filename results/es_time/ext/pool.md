# Candidate pool

136 distinct datasets found by three separate searches over OpenML, UCI, Kaggle, city and government open-data portals, research archives and code repositories, listed once each, ordered by the number of searches that reported them (three, two, one); the order in which each search found them is not kept. The facts a search reported (rows, minority share, span, licence) were not verified at this stage. The fixed list and a shortlist of finalists then went through the licence gate and the pre-run checks (see `candidates.md`); the other entries were not examined. The web-search queries the searches recorded are in `search_queries.md`; much of the pool came from reading catalogues directly (OpenML and UCI listings, Kaggle dataset pages, portal metadata), which has no query.
