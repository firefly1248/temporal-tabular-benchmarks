# Extension candidates and licence table

Sixteen datasets enter the extension. Licence gate: publishing aggregate and per-dataset metrics from a non-commercial analysis, with attribution, must be allowed by the owner's licence or terms; a dataset whose terms could not be read, or that needs consent, is rejected. Licence texts were read from the owners' pages on 2026-11-20 and the quotes below are verbatim unless a cell says otherwise; where a page could not be read the cell says so. A page that could not be read counts as a rejection, except where a conditional pass is marked and the unread upstream terms are named. Pre-run checks (1-5) use the default-LightGBM time-split on the thin 10,000-row variant, no arm of the study was fitted. The candidate pool was 136 datasets found by web searches over OpenML, UCI, Kaggle, city and government portals and research archives (queries in `search_queries.md`, the pool in `pool.md`); the fixed list and a shortlist of finalists were gated, the rest were not examined.
